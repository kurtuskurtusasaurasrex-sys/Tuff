TOURNAMENT

CONTROLS

Click a box, then press the key you want.

One player on the keyboard can use any of these plus J/Z/Space, K/X, L/C/Shift. Gamepad: stick or D-pad, A/X attack, B/Y special, bumpers guard.

HOW TO PLAY

Only 4 controls. Knock everyone off the slab - the more damage a fighter has, the farther they fly.

MOVEWASD / Arrows - walk in every direction, including up and down the arena
ATTACKtap for a 3-hit combo. Hold a direction + Attack for a heavy strike
SPECIALranged move. Direction + Special = movement special. Meter full = SUPER
GUARDhold to block (tap just before a hit = perfect guard). Direction + Guard = dodge roll
  • DAMAGE % raises knockback. Walls don't exist - the edge of the slab does.
  • METER fills as you fight. At 50% press Guard while stunned to BREAK out. At 100% unleash your SUPER.
  • Pop the floating SMASH BALL with attacks to fill your meter instantly.
  • 3 stocks each (change it in the lobby). Last one standing wins, or the lowest damage when time runs out.
  • Up to 4 players: friends online, friends on gamepads, or CPUs. Touch screens get an on-screen stick and buttons.
