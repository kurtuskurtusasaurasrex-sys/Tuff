HOW TO PLAY

Only 4 controls. Knock your rival off the slab - the more damage they have, the farther they fly.

MOVEWASD / Arrows - walk in every direction, including up and down the arena
ATTACKJ / Z / Space - tap for a 3-hit combo. Hold a direction + Attack for a heavy strike
SPECIALK / X - ranged move. Direction + Special = movement special. Meter full = SUPER
GUARDL / C / Shift - hold to block (tap just before a hit = perfect guard). Direction + Guard = dodge roll
  • DAMAGE % raises knockback. Land hits, then send them over the edge - walls don't exist, the slab does.
  • METER fills as you fight. At 50% hit Guard while stunned to BREAK out and fly back to the middle. At 100% unleash your SUPER.
  • Each fighter has 3 stocks. Last one standing, or lowest damage when time runs out, wins.
  • Local 2-player: P1 = WASD + F/G/H, P2 = Arrows + K/L/;  ·  Gamepads work too. Touch screens get on-screen buttons.
